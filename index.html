About Me

I am an undergraduate researcher at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen . My research interests focus on urban analytics, human mobility, spatial interaction, social sensing, and geospatial big data.

I am particularly interested in understanding how physical and institutional boundaries shape urban mobility and spatial interaction, and in developing quantitative geographic methods for representing and explaining these effects.
